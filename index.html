Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I get a quotation?

Send us your design requirements, approximate quantity, intended platform and deadline through the inquiry form. We’ll discuss the scope and provide a quotation before starting.

Can you design in Sinhala and English?

Yes. Tell us which language or combination of languages you need and provide the final wording when you contact us.

Are the portfolio examples real client projects?

The seven items currently shown are concept samples created by CS Chapters to demonstrate possible styles. They are not presented as paid client work.

What information should I send?

A short description of your business, the design you need, your target audience, preferred colours or references, wording, image assets and required delivery date will help us understand the project.
